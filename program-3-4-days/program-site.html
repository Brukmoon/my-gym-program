How to run it

Effort

Finish most sets with 1–3 reps left. No grinding. Sessions take about 60–75 min; drop the optional items first.

Progress

Top of the rep range on every set → add weight next time (+2.5 kg upper, +5 kg lower), or move to a harder variation.

Plyos & Nordics

Plyos are about quality: stop a set when contacts feel slow. Nordics: add at most one rep per week.

Lighter weeks

Every 5th–6th week, or a race week: same exercises, about half the sets. In high-mileage blocks, 2–3 sets on lower days.
