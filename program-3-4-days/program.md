# Program – 3–4 days/week

Goal: get stronger overall and run more efficiently, alongside cycling and running.
Built from your existing lower / upper-back / upper-front plus the missing pieces.
Swap freely (see "Swaps" at the bottom). Just keep the weekly checklist ticked.

Notation: sets x reps. "/leg" or "/side" = per side.
Effort: finish most sets with 1–3 reps left in the tank. No grinding.
Session length: ~60–75 min. Items marked (optional) go first when short on time.

---

## Warm-ups (5–10 min)

**Lower days**
- Tibialis raises (back against wall, lift toes) – 2 x 15–20
- Lateral band walks – 2 x 10 steps/side
- Leg swings, bodyweight lunges – 1 round

**Upper days**
- Band external rotations (elbow at 90°, pinned to side) – 2 x 15
- Y-raises (face down on incline bench, light) – 2 x 10
- Push-up plus – 1 x 10

## Example week (4 days + running/cycling)

| Day | Gym | Cardio |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Lower A | Easy run (before gym or separately) |
| Tue | Upper B | Easy ride |
| Wed | – | **Hard run** (intervals / tempo) |
| Thu | Lower B | – |
| Fri | Upper A | Easy run or ride |
| Sat | – | **Long run or long ride** |
| Sun | Rest | |

Rules:
- Leave ~48 h between a heavy lower day and a hard or long run.
- If you have to put them on the same day, do the hard run first (AM) and lift later (PM).
- Upper days can go anywhere.

---

## Progression

- **Main lifts:** when you hit the top of the rep range on all sets, add weight next time (+2.5 kg upper, +5 kg lower).
- **Bodyweight moves:** add reps → make harder (pistol to lower box, archer push-ups, handstand vs pike, weighted pull-ups/dips).
- **Nordics:** progress slowly (+1 rep per week max). Hamstrings get very sore at first.
- **Plyometrics:** it's about quality, not fatigue. Stop a set as soon as contacts feel slow or heavy.
- **Deload:** every 5th–6th week, or in a race week: same exercises, about half the sets, keep the weight.
- **High-mileage blocks:** drop to 2–3 sets on lower days and keep the intensity. Keep the plyos and nordics.

## Weekly checklist (when you mix things up)

- [ ] 1+ heavy squat or deadlift
- [ ] 2+ single-leg exercises
- [ ] 2 plyometric blocks
- [ ] Nordics (or slider hamstring curls) ~2x
- [ ] Side-hip work (Copenhagen / side plank leg raise / band walks)
- [ ] Both calf types (bent-knee + straight-knee) + tibialis raises
- [ ] Pulling sets ≥ pushing sets
- [ ] Rotator cuff + Y-raises (warm-ups count)
- [ ] Some anti-rotation / hanging core
